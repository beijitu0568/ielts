Q03

查看答案

Yes, quite often. Especially when I'm out walking or working out, music can make these moments more fun. But not when I need to concentrate on something like studying, because it would distract me and break my focus.

Q01

查看答案

Yeah, I sure do. I really prefer to keep my room neat. A tidy space can put me in a good mood and help me stay focused when I study or work. But if I’m already worn out, I can’t be bothered to tidy up.

Q02

查看答案

Not really. I hardly ever tidied my room as a kid. I often left books and clothes everywhere. My mum was always complaining about how messy it was. But in the end, she usually tidied it up for me.

Q03

查看答案

Well, nothing special really. I usually just wipe my desk and sweep the floor. And I generally do this before I start studying. You know, it’s just my little ritual before I get down to work.

Q04

Do you think that it is necessary to be tidy?

查看答案

I’d say it is. A tidy space can put people in a good mood and help them stay focused. Besides, it saves plenty of time as they can find what they need much faster. When I’m running late, I really don’t want to play hide-and-seek with my keys.

Q05

Do you think there are people who can always stay tidy?

查看答案

Yeah definitely. My grandma is one of those people. She always keeps her home neat and orderly. Once, when some guests came over and sat on her bed, she washed the bed sheets right after they left. To me, that was really unbelievable.

Q06

What do you think of the people who are not very tidy?

查看答案

Well, I don’t judge them at all. I think it’s mostly just a personal habit. Some creative types tend to be a little messy, but they often know exactly where their things are. I’m not very tidy myself, so I can totally relate.
